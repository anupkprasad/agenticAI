Salt‑bridge analysis highlights a persistent Lys1003–Glu1009 interaction, consistent with the hinge‑region network reported for JAK2 activation [3].

Overall, the simulations support a picture of JAK2 as a structurally rigid kinase that maintains ATP engagement through a stable pocket architecture, yet permits sufficient flexibility in the activation loop to accommodate regulatory phosphorylation events.
