The inter‑lobe DCCM mean correlations further distinguish the two groups; active kinases show a strong positive correlation (≈ 0.6) between N‑ and C‑lobes, reflecting coordinated domain motions, while pseudokinases exhibit weak or negative correlations (≈ 0.1–0.3), suggestive of decoupled domain dynamics. The shared‑reference dihedral PCA scalar (pca_pka_ref_shared_dyn) places EGFR, JAK2, and CDK2 closest to the KAPCA reference (values < 0.4), whereas TRIB2 and KSR1/2 lie farther apart (> 0.8), mirroring their divergent catalytic architecture.
