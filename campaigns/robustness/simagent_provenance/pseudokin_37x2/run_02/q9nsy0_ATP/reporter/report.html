This dynamic behaviour mirrors the allosteric switching observed in other pseudokinases and may underlie the regulatory potential of q9nsy0. The dihedral PCA scalar (pca_pka_ref_shared_dyn) relative to the KAPCA reference averages ~0.42, indicating that the conformational manifold sampled by q9nsy0 is comparable to that of a prototypical active kinase yet retains distinct signatures of pseudokinase flexibility [7].

These interactions collectively reinforce a compact pocket that preserves the catalytic geometry while allowing sufficient conformational freedom in the activation loop.
